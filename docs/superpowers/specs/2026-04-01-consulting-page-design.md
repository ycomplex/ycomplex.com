# Design: Consulting Page

## Summary

Add a first-person consulting page to the YComplex website, linked from the homepage tagline ("Consulting"). Also add a "Book a Conversation" CTA to the homepage.

## Files

- `consulting.html` — new page
- `index.html` — add booking link below email, update tagline link

## Consulting Page (`consulting.html`)

Shared design system: dark bg, mesh (30 nodes), Syne + IBM Plex Mono, nav, corners. Single-column scrollable layout, max-width ~700px centered.

### Sections

**1. Hero**
- Heading: "Consulting"
- One-liner subtitle, e.g. "I help leaders turn technology from a question mark into a competitive advantage."

**2. How I Work** (the lead section)
- 2-3 short first-person paragraphs
- Core message: embeds directly with leadership — sometimes strategist, sometimes builder, often both. No layers, no juniors, no handoffs. Advises on direction and builds the proof of concept. Understands business context when building.
- Covers strategy, implementation, advisory, training fluidly — not as a service catalog

**3. Who I Work With**
- Three compact lines: Founders & startups / Mid-market PE portfolio companies / Enterprise leadership teams
- Note on industry depth: Technology, Financial Services, Telecoms — but not limited

**4. Background**
- Scannable block, not prose:
  - 25 years in technology and strategy
  - Stanford CS (BS + MS), London Business School MBA
  - Head of Strategy, Skype · Global Head of Digital, Vodafone · Associate Partner, McKinsey
  - Leadership roles at Microsoft, JP Morgan, Morgan Stanley, Cisco

**5. CTA**
- Gold filled button: "Book a Conversation" → https://calendar.app.google/tq7YUogLHbEvEDQ2A
- Below: "Or email contact@ycomplex.com" as subtle text link
